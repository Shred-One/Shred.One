# Support

## Need help?

Email support@shred.one for account, payment or delivery help.

[Email support](mailto:support@shred.one)

Include the relevant account email, service ID, transaction signature, destination and approximate time. Never send a private key, password, session token or magic link.

Shred.one support does not use a ticket portal, live chat or public status page in the MVP.
