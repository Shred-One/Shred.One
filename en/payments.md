# Payments

Each account has one fixed deposit address, one shared USDC balance and an append-only balance ledger.

## Supported payment

Send only native USDC on Solana mainnet to the deposit address shown in Account. A payment is credited after it reaches finalized status.

## Payment finalization

Finalization can take time. A payment only credits your balance; it never creates, activates or renews a service by itself.

## Founding qualification

Check [current pricing and Founding enrollment status](https://shred.one/#pricing-title), including the qualifying single-payment amount, before paying. If current pricing cannot be confirmed, do not assume new Founding enrollment is open.

The rollout milestone for the Founding program is full deployment of all four regions. Only a qualifying single payment recorded while enrollment is open grants new Founding status. A payment recorded after enrollment closes does not grant new Founding status. The full payment is credited to your account balance; it is not an extra fee.

Founding status, once granted, is permanent for your account and applies to every current and future service and region, even if subscriptions stop, expire, or are interrupted. Existing Founding accounts keep their rate after enrollment closes. Multiple smaller payments are not combined for qualification.
